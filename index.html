Privacy Terms

Clean up your mailbox, one click at a time.

EmailHandler is a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux. It sorts every email into a colored tag, finds subscriptions you can leave, and suggests what is safe to delete. It works with Gmail and Outlook, and nothing changes in your mailbox until you click.

🏷️

Tags

Every email gets a colored tag: billing, orders, newsletters, promotions and more.

📭

Unsubscribe

Find mailing lists and leave them with the sender's official unsubscribe link.

🧹

Safe to delete

Careful suggestions for old and spam emails. Starred and flagged emails are left out of them.

🔒

Stays on your computer

No EmailHandler server. Sign-in tokens are encrypted by your operating system.

How it decides

EmailHandler uses Jev, a decision model by TypeSafe, with your own TypeSafe API key. Read exactly what is sent and stored in the Privacy Policy.
