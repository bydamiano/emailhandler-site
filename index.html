Clean up your mailbox, one click at a time.

A free desktop app that sorts every email into a colored tag, finds subscriptions you can leave, and suggests what is safe to delete. Works with Gmail and Outlook. Nothing changes in your mailbox until you click.

Beta: invited testers only

While Google reviews the app, Gmail sign-in only works for invited testers, and Google shows “Google hasn't verified this app” (click Advanced → Go to EmailHandler). Outlook and Hotmail accounts can sign in already. Want to try it with Gmail? Write to bydamiano07@gmail.com with your Gmail address.

Install on macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag EmailHandler into Applications.
  2. Open EmailHandler. macOS says it can't verify the developer, because the app is free and not notarized by Apple. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to EmailHandler. You only do this once.
  4. Click Sign in with Google or Sign in with Microsoft, then paste your TypeSafe API key (get one here).

Tags

📭

Unsubscribe

Find mailing lists and leave them with the sender's official unsubscribe link.

🧹

Safe to delete

Careful suggestions for old and spam emails. Starred and flagged emails are left out of them.

🔒

Stays on your computer

No EmailHandler server. Sign-in tokens are encrypted by your operating system.

How it decides

EmailHandler uses Jev, a decision model by TypeSafe, with your own TypeSafe API key. Read exactly what is sent and stored in the Privacy Policy, or everything the app does in the README.
