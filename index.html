Power-ups

Seven power-ups for a tidy inbox.

All unlocked from day one. On as many Gmail and Outlook accounts as you like, with one TypeSafe key for all of them.

  • Starter

    Tags every email

    Billing, Orders, Newsletters, Promotions, Social, Work, Personal, Travel, Security, Spam or Other. In your language, in your real mailbox.

  • Rare

    Finds what matters

    An Important tag for receipts, contracts, prescriptions and real conversations, next to the normal tag.

  • Epic

    Finds what's safe to delete

    Promotions, expired alerts, old delivery updates. Emails older than 6 months get an "importance today" score from 0 to 4.

  • Legendary

    Unsubscribes in bulk

    One click when the sender supports it, an email when it doesn't, a link as the last resort. Never for spam.

  • Epic

    Cleans up by sender

    Tag or trash everything from one sender in a period, with the exact count shown first.

  • Rare

    Empties the Trash

    One click, permanent, with a confirmation first. The only thing that ever deletes for good.

  • Rare

    Several accounts

    Gmail and Outlook side by side. Switch with one click from the account menu.

  • Always on

    Rule zero: nothing happens on its own.

    Every action needs your click and shows the exact number of emails first. Starred and flagged mail is never touched.

Privacy

Your mail stays on your computer.

There is no EmailHandler server. The app talks to your mailbox and to Jev directly, with your own sign-in and your own key.

  • Sign-ins and your TypeSafe key are encrypted by your operating system (Keychain, DPAPI, libsecret).
  • Scan results stay in a local database, one per account. Email bodies are never stored.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry. Read the full privacy policy.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Send a message.

Is EmailHandler free?

Yes. The app is free. To decide what each email is, it uses Jev, TypeSafe's decision model, with your own TypeSafe API key. TypeSafe bills that usage directly; it costs a fraction of a cent per email.

Can EmailHandler change or delete my email without asking?

No. Scans only read and suggest. Tagging, moving to Trash and unsubscribing happen only after you click, and you see the exact number of emails first. Deleted emails go to Trash; only Empty Trash deletes for good, after a confirmation.

Where does my email go?

The app talks directly to Gmail or Outlook with your own sign-in, and to TypeSafe with your own key. There is no EmailHandler server. Results stay on your computer and sign-ins are encrypted by your operating system.

Which email accounts does it work with?

Gmail and Google Workspace, and Outlook.com, Hotmail, Live, MSN and Microsoft 365. You can connect several accounts.

Why does my computer warn me when I open EmailHandler?

The app is free and isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate. On macOS open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. On Windows click More info, then Run anyway. You do it once.
